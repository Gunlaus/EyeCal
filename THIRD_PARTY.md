## Generated blue-noise texture

The EyeCal 16 × 16 rank texture is generated by [the included script](assets/blue-noise/generate.py) from a seeded point set and a periodic Gaussian kernel. The generator follows the void-and-cluster ranking method described by Robert Ulichney. No external texture is copied. The packed rank texture is dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). [Original algorithm paper](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.152707)
