# EyeCal

**Calibrate your display by eye, even if you've never calibrated anything before.**

EyeCal is a small fullscreen app that walks you through your display's settings
one at a time: black level, white clip, gamma, sharpness, banding and uniformity.
Each screen shows one test pattern, tells you what it should look like, and shows
what a bad result looks like, so you know when you've got it right.

No colorimeter or technical knowledge needed. Download, double-click, follow the prompts.
